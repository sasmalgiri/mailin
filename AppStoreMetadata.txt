DESCRIPTION
------------------------------------

mailin is the only forensic-grade email archive analyzer that runs natively on iPhone, iPad, AND Mac — with zero cloud, zero tracking, and zero third-party SDKs.

Open Gmail Takeout .mbox, Outlook .pst/.msg, Apple Mail .emlx, Lotus Notes .nsf, or any RFC 822 .eml file. Search across millions of messages with sub-second full-text search. Analyze with on-device Apple Intelligence. Export with forensic chain-of-custody artifacts, Bates numbering, and cryptographic signatures.

Other email tools force you to choose: Mac-only (MailSteward), Windows-only (Aid4Mail, Intella, MailXaminer), or cloud upload (Relativity, Logikcull). mailin is none of those. Universal Apple-native. Strictly on-device. Verifiable in App Sandbox.

IMPORT & BROWSE
- Open .mbox, .eml, .emlx, .msg, .pst, .ost, .nsf, and .zip archives from any email provider
- Full MIME and RFC 822 support with attachment handling
- Conversation threading groups related emails together
- Full-text search across subjects, senders, and body content
- Import from Gmail Takeout, Thunderbird, Apple Mail, Outlook, Lotus Notes
