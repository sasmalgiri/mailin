DESCRIPTION
------------------------------------

mailin is a powerful email archive analyzer built with 100% native Apple technology. Free to try. No ads. No data collection. Ever.

FLEXIBLE PRICING — YOUR CHOICE
Try mailin free with up to 50 emails. Upgrade to Pro with a monthly subscription, yearly plan, or a one-time lifetime purchase. No pressure, no tricks — choose what works for you.

COMPLETE PRIVACY — ZERO DATA LEAVES YOUR DEVICE
mailin processes everything locally using Apple's on-device AI and NLP frameworks. No cloud servers. No telemetry. No accounts required. No internet connection needed. Your emails are yours alone.

NATIVE APPLE. EVERY DEVICE.
Built entirely in SwiftUI with Apple's native frameworks — not Electron, not Java, not a web wrapper. Runs natively on every Mac with Apple Silicon and Intel. Fast, fluid, and designed for macOS.

KEY FEATURES:

- On-Device AI Assistant: Ask natural language questions about your emails — sentiment analysis, topic extraction, entity recognition, language detection — all powered by Apple's NaturalLanguage framework running entirely on your device.

- Advanced Email Parsing: Full RFC 822 and MIME compliant parser that handles complex multipart messages, nested attachments, quoted-printable, base64, and international character sets.

- Smart Filtering: Filter emails by sender, recipient, date range, domain, reply count, and message type. Find exactly what you need in seconds.

- Reply Analytics: Track communication patterns with reply frequency charts, conversation threading, and sender statistics.

- Attachment Management: View, download, and export email attachments — PDF, images, documents, and more. Batch download all attachments at once.

- Multiple Export Formats: Export filtered emails as EML, JSON, CSV, or plain text for backup, legal discovery, or further analysis.

- Google Takeout Ready: Import .mbox files exported from Gmail via Google Takeout. Also supports Thunderbird, Apple Mail, and any standard .mbox or .eml files.

PERFECT FOR:
- Professionals managing email archives
- Legal teams and compliance officers
- Researchers analyzing communication patterns
- Anyone leaving Gmail who wants to read their Google Takeout data
- Privacy-conscious users who refuse to upload emails to cloud services
