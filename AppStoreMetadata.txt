DESCRIPTION
------------------------------------

mailin is a powerful, privacy-first email archive analyzer for macOS. Import your .mbox or .eml email archives and instantly gain insights into your communication patterns.

KEY FEATURES:

- Advanced Email Parsing: Full RFC 822 and MIME compliant parser that handles complex multipart messages, nested attachments, and encoded content.

- Smart Filtering: Filter emails by sender, recipient, date range, message type (sent/received), and more. Find exactly what you're looking for in seconds.

- Reply Analytics: Track your communication patterns with detailed reply frequency statistics and conversation threading.

- AI Email Assistant: Ask natural language questions about your email archive — "Who did I email the most?" "What are my most common subjects?"

- Attachment Management: View, save, and open email attachments directly from the analyzer. Supports PDF, images, documents, and more.

- Privacy First: All processing happens entirely on your Mac. No data is sent to any server. Your emails stay on your device.

- Export Capabilities: Export filtered emails in EML, JSON, or plain text formats for backup or further analysis.

Perfect for professionals, researchers, legal teams, and anyone who needs to analyze email archives efficiently.
