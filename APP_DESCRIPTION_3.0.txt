mailin turns exported email into an organized, searchable, defensible case file — on your Mac. No account, no tracking.

Import Gmail Takeout (.mbox), Apple Mail (.emlx), Outlook (.msg/.pst/.ost), Lotus Notes (.nsf), or .eml files and mailin builds a fast, structured archive.

NEW IN 3.0 — EVIDENCE STUDIOS
- Hypothesis Matrix (ACH): score competing explanations against cited emails; ranked by fewest inconsistencies — you decide, never the app
- Fact–Evidence Matrix: map each contested fact to supporting AND opposing emails; both sides preserved
- Reasoning Studio: 5W1H, Five Whys, Fishbone, and root-cause analysis — every cell cites emails or says UNKNOWN
- Evidence Desks: Admiralty source-reliability grid (seeded from SPF/DKIM signals), contradiction and gap registers
- Action Register: corrective actions linked to causes; closing requires a named human verifier with evidence
- Sealed documents: every numbered work product carries a cryptographic seal — any later edit fails verification
- Sealed case handoff: export a whole case as a verifiable .mailincase bundle; tampered bundles are refused
- Team review: merge a colleague's work with attribution — conflicts kept side by side, never silently merged
- New Researcher persona: protocols, screening, extraction & coding, cited chronologies
