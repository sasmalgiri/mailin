mailin turns exported email into an organized, searchable, defensible case file — on your Mac. No account, no tracking.

Import Gmail Takeout (.mbox), Apple Mail (.emlx), Outlook (.msg/.pst/.ost), Lotus Notes (.nsf), or .eml files and mailin builds a fast, structured archive.

GUIDED JOBS, NOT JUST TOOLS
Pick the job you came to do and mailin walks you through it step by step — saving your work as you go and producing a numbered document you can reopen, export to CSV, and pull into reports.
- 47 built-in workflows across 5 roles: Forensic, Legal/eDiscovery, IT, Journalist, Personal
- Auto-save, sequential step gates, sign-off, and who/when attestation
- Attach the exact files and archive emails as evidence to each step
- A numbered document trail (issue, notes, reversal) tied to a tamper-evident log

SEARCH AND BROWSE
- Full-text and structured search across subjects, senders, and body content
- Conversation threading, smart filters, and Gmail label detection from Takeout
- Command palette and a macOS menu-bar quick search

ANALYSIS AND INSIGHTS
- Analytics dashboard: volume timeline, top contacts, activity heatmap, attachment mix
- Communication patterns, relationship/entity network graphs, topic clusters
- Thread Story: a one-click narrative of any thread, grounded to only its emails
- Duplicate and near-duplicate detection with a review pass
- Anomaly detection and smart alerts for unusual senders, off-hours, and auth failures
- PII detection and GDPR data-subject-access reporting with a redaction pass
- Executive dashboard for a case-level overview

ON-DEVICE AI
- AI assistant that cites the exact emails behind every answer
- On-device via Apple Intelligence (macOS 26+); sentiment, topics, keywords, language detection
- Optional weekly digest of your saved searches
- Optional Cloud AI mode is opt-in and off by default, using your own provider key

EXPORT
- Per-email: EML, PDF, TIFF, MSG

- Forensic reports with SHA-256 hash manifests
