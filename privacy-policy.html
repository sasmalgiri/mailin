Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

Overview

mailin ("the App") is an email archive analyzer for Mac, iPhone, and iPad. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Data Collection

mailin does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

All email parsing, analysis, and AI processing happens entirely on your device. The App does not send any information to us or any third party.

Data You Provide

The App processes email archive files (.mbox, .eml, .emlx, .msg, .pst, .ost, .nsf, and .zip) that you choose to open. These files are read locally on your device and are never uploaded or shared. The App accesses only files you explicitly select through the system file picker.

Data Storage

Imported email data is stored locally on your device using Apple's standard app container. Your preferences (such as your default email address and display settings) are stored locally using standard preferences. You can clear all saved data at any time from Settings. When you clear data, it is permanently deleted.

Network Access

mailin does not require an internet connection. Network activity is limited to Apple's StoreKit for in-app purchases and subscription verification, handled entirely by Apple's systems.

Third-Party Services

mailin does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

All transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store. mailin does not have access to your payment information.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
