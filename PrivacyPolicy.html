mailin Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 29, 2026

mailin does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Period.

Data Collection

mailin collects zero user data. We do not collect analytics, usage data, crash reports, device information, or any other personal information.

Email Processing

All email parsing, analysis, and AI processing happens entirely on your device. Your email data is never uploaded to any server, cloud service, or third party. mailin uses Apple's on-device NaturalLanguage framework and FoundationModels framework for AI features — both run locally on your Mac.

Network Access

mailin does not require an internet connection to function. The only network activity is related to Apple's StoreKit for in-app purchases, which is handled entirely by Apple's systems.

Third-Party Services

mailin does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple's privacy policy governs how purchase transactions are handled. mailin does not have access to your payment information.

Children's Privacy

mailin does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update this page. Given that we collect no data, we do not anticipate changes.
