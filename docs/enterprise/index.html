What your teams get

Everything in mailin Professional, in one purchase per person — no in-app purchases, no accounts with us.

📂 Every archive format

MBOX, EML, EMLX, MSG, PST, OST, OLM, NSF and ZIP, with folders, labels and attachments kept. Large archives stream from disk; every import ends with a receipt.

🔎 Search inside attachments

Text inside PDF, Office and iWork files and scanned images; AND / OR / NOT, dates, senders, Whole words. Every result says where it matched and how much was searched.

⚖️ Legal and compliance tools

Custodians, legal holds, review batches, redaction, Bates numbering, productions to PST, MBOX, EML, PDF and CSV — each export read back and verified.

🛡️ Tamper-evident by design

SHA-256 on every message, an HMAC-chained audit log, Ed25519-signed exports and sealed case bundles. Any later edit fails verification, and a third party can check it without your machines.

📬 Live Mail, preserved

Optionally preserve Microsoft 365 / Outlook, Gmail and IMAP mailboxes into the archive, kept even if the server later deletes them. The device talks directly to your mail provider — never to us.

🧠 On-device AI only

Summaries and questions run on Apple's on-device models; counts come from the archive, not the model. No cloud AI, no telemetry, no analytics.

Controlled by your IT

Standard managed app configuration through Jamf, Intune, Kandji, Mosyle, Workspace ONE or Apple Business Manager. A setting you fix cannot be changed in the app, and it is enforced where the app connects — not only on screen.

You setWhat happens
allowConnectedMailSwitch Live Mail off: no mail connection at all, the archive keeps working.
allowedMailProvidersOnly Microsoft 365, Gmail, iCloud, Fastmail or IMAP — any mix you choose.
allowSendMail · allowServerChangesPreserve only: nothing is sent, nothing on the server is moved or deleted.
syncOnlyOnWiFi · syncOnlyWhileCharging · maxDownloadMBpsWhen and how fast mailboxes are downloaded.
role · allowExport · allowPrintViewer, Reviewer, Examiner or Administrator; exporting and printing allowed or not.
requireBiometricLock · disabledModules · orgName · examinerNameForced app lock, pages switched off for everyone, your name on reports and receipts.

The app reports its version, the settings in force and any configuration problem back to your MDM — never anything from the archive. Ready-made Jamf schema, AppConfig XML and a sample macOS profile are in the deployment guide.
