Why local-first is the enterprise feature

The security review most SaaS tools fail is one mailin never has to take: your case data never leaves your devices.

🔒 Zero egress by default

Import, search, analysis, AI — all on-device. The one optional cloud feature (bring-your-own-key AI) can be disabled fleet-wide by MDM policy, a hard gate no user toggle can override.

🛡️ Tamper-evident by design

HMAC-chained audit logs, Ed25519-sealed work products, and sealed case bundles. Any edit after posting fails verification loudly — and a third party can verify without contacting your machines.

🖥️ MDM-managed

Standard Managed App Configuration: preset examiner identity, enforce the app lock, disable cloud AI org-wide, set case-number prefixes. Works with Jamf, Intune, Kandji, Mosyle.

🤝 Team handoff without a server

Sealed .mailincase bundles carry a case between examiners; imports verify the seal before opening and merge with per-examiner attribution — conflicts kept, never silently merged.

⚖️ Evidence discipline

Every finding cites its emails; contested facts keep both sides; the app never asserts guilt or closes an action — humans decide, on the record.

🏢 Apple Business Manager

Distributed as an Apple Custom App: your organization buys managed licenses in ABM and deploys through your MDM. All Professional features included — no in-app purchases.
