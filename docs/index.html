Powerful features, completely private

Everything runs on your Mac. No data ever leaves your device.

On-Device AI Assistant

Ask natural language questions about your emails. Powered by Apple NaturalLanguage and Apple Intelligence on macOS 26+.

  • Sentiment analysis for every email
  • Topic & keyword extraction
  • Language detection
  • Priority scoring
  • AI-powered smart filters
  • PII & GDPR compliance scanning
🔍

Advanced Search & Filters

Find any email in seconds with powerful search across subjects, senders, body content, and attachments.

  • Boolean operators (AND / OR / NOT)
  • Regex and wildcard search
  • Proximity search
  • Filter by sender, domain, date range
  • Gmail label detection
  • Attachment, flagged, unread filters
📊

Analytics Dashboard

Visualize communication patterns with interactive charts and statistics.

  • Email volume timeline
  • Top contacts & communication pairs
  • Activity heatmap by day and hour
  • Attachment type breakdown
  • Contact network visualization
  • Exportable analytics reports
🔒

Forensic & Legal Tools

Professional-grade tools for legal discovery, compliance, and forensic investigation.

  • SPF, DKIM, DMARC authentication
  • Spoofing & phishing detection
  • Evidence tagging & annotations
  • Chain of custody tracking
  • Tamper-proof audit trail
  • Bates numbering for legal docs
📦

Flexible Export

Export your emails and analysis in any format you need.

  • EML, CSV, JSON, PDF, plain text
  • MSG & PST export
  • Bates-stamped PDF & TIFF
  • vCard contact export
  • Forensic reports (SHA-256, SHA-1, MD5)
  • Concordance load files
  • Redacted exports (PII removed)
  • Bulk attachment download
💬

Smart Email Viewing

Browse and read emails with a native, fast interface designed for Mac.

  • Conversation threading
  • HTML & plain text rendering
  • RTF body rendering
  • Inline image display
  • S/MIME signature verification
  • Attachment preview & Quick Look

Batch Processing

Handle large archives efficiently with tools designed for bulk operations.

  • Deduplication detection
  • Custodian management
  • Legal hold marking
  • Review batching
  • Predictive coding (AI)
  • Near-duplicate detection
