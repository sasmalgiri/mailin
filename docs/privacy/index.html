Privacy Policy

Local archive and search. Optional connected mail, directly with your own provider.

Effective Date: November 14, 2026 (version 4.0). The policy that applied to version 3.0 is kept here for the record.

mailin collects no personal data. There is no mailin server: your archive, your mail and your sign-in details stay on your device or go directly to the mail provider you chose.

Two editions

Connected edition (mailin 4.0 on the App Store). Adds optional connected mail for Gmail, Outlook / Microsoft 365, iCloud Mail, Fastmail and other IMAP / SMTP accounts. Connected mail is off until you turn it on, and each account is allowed nothing until you switch on, one by one, what it may do.

Offline edition (mailin 3.x, and offline builds made for organisations). Its app sandbox grants no network access and it offers no way to add an account, so it cannot connect to any server. Everything on this page about connected mail does not apply to it.

The About window in the app says which edition you have.

Data Collection

mailin collects no user data in either edition. We do not collect analytics, usage data, crash reports, device information, contact lists, email content or any other personal information, and we have no server that could receive it. Nothing you import, save, search or send reaches the developer.

Email Processing

All parsing, search, duplicate detection, exports and analysis happen on your device. AI features use Apple's on-device frameworks only.

No Cloud AI

Neither edition contains cloud AI. No email content, attachment, search or summary is sent to any AI provider. Mail captured from Gmail is never used for AI outside your device.

Connected Mail (connected edition only)

When you turn on Connected mail and add an account, your device connects directly to that account's own mail provider. Nothing is relayed through a mailin server or any other intermediary. Each account does only what you allowed it:

  • Preserve mail (read-only): downloads your messages, folders and labels and keeps a copy in your archive on this device. Nothing on the server is changed and nothing is marked as read.
  • Send mail: sends a message you wrote and submitted from the outbox, from that account's own address.
  • Change server mail: applies changes you choose (read / unread, flag, move or label, move to Trash, restore). Each change is listed first; a bulk cleanup runs only after you type the exact count shown.

Your mail provider processes your mail under its own terms and privacy policy, as it does for any mail app. Messages are shown with remote content (images, tracking pixels) blocked, so opening a message does not contact the sender.

Sign-in and Credentials

Gmail connects with a Google app password; mailin never asks for your Google account password. Outlook / Microsoft 365 sign-in happens on Microsoft's own page; mailin never sees your Microsoft password. The sign-in tokens the provider issues, and any app password or IMAP / SMTP password you enter, are stored only in this device's Keychain. They are never synchronised to other devices, never included in backups or exports, and never sent anywhere except to the provider that issued them. A restored or new device asks you to sign in again.

Google API Services (applies only when Google sign-in ships)

Version 4.0 uses no Google API: it connects Gmail over IMAP with an app password, as described above. Google sign-in is planned for a later 4.0.x update, after Google has verified the app; this page will be updated before that update is released, and the section below applies only from then, to accounts added with Google sign-in.

When Google sign-in is offered, mailin's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to provide the features you switched on, stays on your device, is not transferred to anyone, is not used for advertising and is not read by any person at mailin.

Removing an Account and Deleting Data

  • To remove an account, open the Mail page, choose the account and Remove account…. Remove stops its connections, cancels outbox messages that were not yet sent (nothing is sent) and deletes its sign-in data (password or tokens) from the Keychain; mail already saved in your archive is kept. Remove and Delete Mail… also deletes that account's saved mail from your archive, after showing the number of messages first (mail under a legal hold, or also saved from another account or import, is kept).
  • To also withdraw mailin's access at the provider, revoke the app password in your Google Account (Security › App passwords) or remove mailin in your Microsoft account (app permissions). mailin cannot do this for you because it has no server.
  • Turning Connected mail off stops every mail connection at once; saved mail stays readable, searchable and exportable.
  • You can delete all data at any time in Settings › Data & Privacy › Delete All Data. This also deletes every connected account's sign-in data from this device's Keychain.

Network Access

The offline edition has no network access: its app sandbox grants no network entitlement, so the system refuses any connection it might attempt. The connected edition (version 4.0) may open connections only to the mail and sign-in servers listed above, only for accounts you added and for what you allowed; every attempt is checked first and can be seen in Settings › Health › Mail network. Apple's StoreKit handles in-app purchases in Apple's systems.

Third-Party Services

mailin integrates no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking or AI services. In the connected edition, the only third parties your device talks to are the mail providers of the accounts you add.

In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

All transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple's privacy policy governs purchase and subscription data. mailin does not have access to your payment information. Subscription status is verified locally through Apple's StoreKit framework.

Spotlight Indexing

mailin optionally indexes email subjects and senders using Apple's CoreSpotlight framework so you can find emails from Spotlight. This index is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted.

Data Storage

Your archive — imported files, mail saved from connected accounts, drafts and the outbox — is a folder you own, stored locally in mailin's app container or, if you move it, on a disk you choose. Moving makes a verified copy at the new location and never deletes the old one until the new one is in use. You can clear all saved data at any time from Settings; cleared data is permanently deleted.

Single messages larger than 100 MB are not copied into the archive. mailin records where the message sits in the file you imported and reads it from there when you open or export it, after checking that the file is still the one that was imported. Keep that file where it is; if it is moved or deleted, mailin says the message's content is unavailable rather than showing an empty message.

Children's Privacy

mailin does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If what the app does with data changes, this page is updated before that version is released.
