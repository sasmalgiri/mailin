Frequently Asked Questions

What file formats does mailin support?

mailin supports .mbox and .eml files. This includes exports from Gmail (Google Takeout), Thunderbird, Apple Mail, and any standard email archive.

Is my data safe?

Yes. mailin processes everything 100% on your device. No data is sent to any server. No cloud, no telemetry, no accounts required.

How do I import my Gmail emails?

Go to Google Takeout, select "Mail", download the export, then open the .mbox file in mailin using "New Import".

What's included in the free version?

The free tier lets you analyze up to 200 emails with all basic features. Upgrade to Personal for unlimited emails and AI, or Professional for forensic tools and audit trail.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open mailin, click "Upgrade" to open the paywall, then click "Restore Purchases" at the bottom.

The app isn't opening my file. What should I do?

Make sure your file has a .mbox or .eml extension. If the file is very large (over 1GB), give it a moment to process. If the issue persists, contact us below.
