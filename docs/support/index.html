Everything you need to know about using mailin.

Getting Started

What is mailin?

mailin is a privacy-first email archive analyzer for Mac, iPhone, and iPad. It lets you import email archives from Gmail, Outlook, Thunderbird, Apple Mail, and other providers, then search, filter, analyze, and export your email history. Available in 11 languages. Everything runs on your device — no cloud servers, no data collection, no account required.

What file formats does mailin support?

mailin supports all major email archive formats:

.mbox — Gmail Takeout, Thunderbird
.eml — Standard email message
.emlx — Apple Mail
.msg — Outlook single email
.pst — Outlook archive
.ost — Outlook offline data
.nsf — Lotus Notes (IBM Notes)

You can also import .zip files containing any of the above formats. mailin will automatically extract and parse them.

How do I import emails?

Open mailin and click "Select Files" or drag and drop files onto the window. You can select one or multiple files at once. mailin will automatically detect the format and begin parsing.

How do I import my Gmail emails?

  1. Go to Google Takeout
  2. Deselect all, then select only "Mail"
  3. Choose your export format (default is fine) and click "Create export"
  4. Wait for Google to prepare your archive (may take hours for large mailboxes)
  5. Download the .zip file when ready
  6. Open mailin and select the .zip file, or extract it and open the .mbox file inside

How do I import from Apple Mail?

Apple Mail stores emails as .emlx files inside ~/Library/Mail/. In mailin, click "Select Files" and navigate to your Mail library to select .emlx files. You can also drag and drop .emlx files from Finder directly into mailin.

How do I import from Outlook?

If you have Outlook .pst or .ost archive files, open them directly in mailin. For individual emails saved as .msg files, select those too. mailin handles all Outlook formats natively.

What are the system requirements?

mailin runs on both Mac and iOS:

  • Mac: macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later — Apple Silicon and Intel
  • iPhone & iPad: iOS 17 / iPadOS 17 or later

Apple Intelligence features require macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later with a supported device.

What languages does mailin support?

mailin's interface is fully localized in 11 languages:

English, Deutsch (German), Español (Spanish), Français (French), हिंदी (Hindi), Italiano (Italian), 日本語 (Japanese), 한국어 (Korean), Português (Brazilian Portuguese), 中文简体 (Chinese Simplified), 中文繁體 (Chinese Traditional).

Your system language is detected automatically. You can also change the language in System Settings > Language & Region.

Features

What AI features does mailin include?

mailin uses Apple's on-device NaturalLanguage framework for:

  • Sentiment analysis — see the emotional tone of every email
  • Topic and keyword extraction — automatically identify key topics
  • Language detection — identify languages in multilingual archives
  • Priority scoring — AI highlights what matters most
  • PII scanning — detect personal information for GDPR compliance
  • AI Assistant — ask natural language questions about your emails
  • AI Smart Filters — describe what you're looking for in plain English

On macOS 26+, mailin also supports Apple Intelligence for enhanced on-device AI capabilities. All AI processing happens entirely on your Mac — nothing is sent to any server.

How does search work?

mailin provides powerful full-text search across subjects, senders, recipients, and email body content. Search features include:

  • Boolean operators — use AND, OR, NOT to combine search terms
  • Wildcard and regex — use * for wildcards or /pattern/ for regex
  • Proximity search — find terms near each other (e.g., "budget" NEAR/5 "deadline")
  • Smart filters — filter by sender, domain, date range, labels, attachments, and more
  • Gmail label detection — automatically reads labels from Google Takeout exports

What analytics are available?

The analytics dashboard shows interactive charts and statistics about your email archive:

  • Email volume timeline with sent vs. received breakdown
  • Top contacts and communication pairs
  • Activity heatmap by day and hour
  • Attachment type breakdown and email size distribution
  • Contact network visualization

Analytics reports can be exported for use in presentations or documentation.

What forensic tools does mailin offer?

Professional-tier forensic tools include:

  • SPF, DKIM, and DMARC analysis — verify email authentication
  • Spoofing and phishing detection — identify suspicious emails
  • Evidence tagging — tag and annotate emails for investigation
  • Chain of custody — track who accessed evidence and when
  • Tamper-proof audit trail — immutable log of all actions
  • Bates numbering — sequential numbering for legal document production
  • SHA-256, SHA-1, and MD5 hashing — verify email integrity
  • MIME tree inspection — examine raw email structure
  • Received chain analysis — trace email routing path

What export formats are available?

mailin can export emails and analysis in multiple formats:

  • EML — standard email format, compatible with any email client
  • CSV — spreadsheet-compatible for data analysis
  • PDF — formatted documents with optional Bates stamping
  • Plain text — simple text export
  • JSON — structured data for developers
  • MSG — Outlook message format export
  • PST — Outlook archive export
  • vCard — export contacts extracted from email headers
  • Forensic reports — with SHA-256, SHA-1, and MD5 hash verification
  • Concordance load files — for legal review platforms
  • Redacted exports — PII automatically removed
  • Bulk attachment download — save all attachments at once

What is conversation threading?

mailin automatically groups related emails into conversation threads based on subject line and message references. This makes it easy to follow back-and-forth exchanges without scrolling through thousands of individual messages.

What other advanced features are available?

mailin includes many additional capabilities beyond the core features:

  • Email comparison — side-by-side diff view of two emails
  • Anomaly detection — automatically flag unusual patterns
  • Keyword monitoring — set up alerts for specific terms
  • Automation rules — auto-tag and organize emails by rules
  • Archive comparison — compare two archive files
  • AI Digest — generate summaries of your email archive
  • Smart alerts — notifications for important patterns
  • Encrypted storage — AES-256 encryption for sensitive archives
  • iCloud sync — sync settings across your Apple devices (via Apple's iCloud, no third-party servers)

Does mailin support Spotlight search?

Yes. mailin can index email subjects and senders using Apple's CoreSpotlight framework, so you can find emails directly from macOS Spotlight search. This index is stored locally and never transmitted. You can disable this in Settings.

How do subscriptions work?

Monthly and yearly subscriptions are auto-renewable. Your Apple ID is charged at the start of each billing period. Subscriptions automatically renew unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

The lifetime option is a one-time purchase — pay once, own it forever. No recurring charges.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime through Apple:

  1. Open System Settings on your Mac
  2. Click your Apple ID at the top
  3. Select Subscriptions
  4. Find mailin and click Cancel Subscription

After cancelling, you'll continue to have access until the end of your current billing period. You will not be charged again.

How do I manage my subscription?

You can manage your subscription in two ways:

  • From mailin: Open the app, go to the upgrade screen, and tap "Manage Subscription"
  • From System Settings: Apple ID → Subscriptions → mailin

From there you can upgrade, downgrade, switch billing periods, or cancel.

How do I restore my purchase?

If you reinstalled mailin or are using a new Mac with the same Apple ID:

  1. Open mailin
  2. Click "Upgrade" to open the paywall
  3. Click "Restore Purchases" at the bottom

Your purchases are tied to your Apple ID and will be restored automatically. This works for both subscriptions and lifetime purchases.

Can I get a refund?

Can I share my purchase with my family?

Privacy & Security

Is my email data safe?

Yes. mailin processes everything 100% on your device. Your email content is never sent to any server, cloud service, or third party. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no tracking, and no account required.

Does mailin need an internet connection?

No. mailin works entirely offline for all email processing, AI analysis, search, and export features. The only network access is for App Store purchases (handled by Apple's StoreKit), which is required to verify subscriptions and process transactions.

Does mailin use cloud AI?

No. All AI features use Apple's on-device NaturalLanguage framework. On macOS 26+, mailin can also use Apple Intelligence's on-device models via the FoundationModels framework. No data is sent to OpenAI, Google, or any other cloud AI service.

Where is my data stored?

All imported email data is stored locally in mailin's sandboxed app container on your Mac. You can clear all data at any time from Settings. When you clear data, it is permanently deleted from your device.

Can I lock mailin with Touch ID?

Yes. mailin supports biometric authentication via Touch ID. You can enable this in Settings to require Touch ID before accessing your email archives.

Troubleshooting

My file isn't opening. What should I do?

Check the following:

  • Make sure your file has a supported extension: .mbox, .eml, .emlx, .msg, .pst, .ost, .nsf, or .zip
  • Try dragging the file directly onto the mailin window instead of using the file picker
  • If the file is inside a .zip, try extracting it first
  • If the file is very large (over 1 GB), give it time to process — mailin uses streaming for large files

If the issue persists, contact us with the file format and size so we can investigate.

How does mailin handle large archives?

mailin uses streaming parsing for large archives. Instead of loading the entire file into memory, it processes emails incrementally. This allows mailin to handle archives of several gigabytes. You'll see a progress indicator while large files are being processed.

Some emails appear garbled or have missing characters.

This usually happens with emails that use non-standard character encodings. mailin supports all major encodings (UTF-8, ISO-8859-1, Windows-1252, and more), but some very old or non-standard emails may not render perfectly. If you encounter this, try exporting the email as EML and opening it in another viewer to verify the original encoding.

AI features aren't working.

The on-device NLP engine requires macOS 14 or later and works on all supported Macs. If AI Smart Filters or the AI Assistant aren't responding:

  • Make sure you haven't exceeded the free-tier limit (3 AI queries for free users)
  • Try closing and reopening mailin
  • Apple Intelligence features (macOS 26+) require a supported device — check Apple's compatibility list

My purchase isn't showing up.

Try these steps in order:

  1. Click "Restore Purchases" in the upgrade screen
  2. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to purchase
  3. Check your subscription status in System Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions
  4. Restart mailin

If the problem continues, contact us with your Apple ID email and approximate purchase date.

Can I use mailin on multiple Macs?

Yes. Your mailin purchase is tied to your Apple ID, not a specific Mac. Sign in with the same Apple ID on any Mac, open mailin, and tap "Restore Purchases" to activate your subscription or lifetime purchase.
